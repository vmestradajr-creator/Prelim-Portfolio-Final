# 03 — SOCIAL MEDIA INFOGRAPHICS & MINI PROJECT

## Overview

The third Prelim activity focused on creating social media infographics and documenting the development of a mini project.

This activity allowed me to apply the design concepts explored in the previous activities while working with a format intended for social media.

The main goal was to communicate information using a visual format that could be understood quickly and clearly.

---

## My Concept

The concept was centered on transforming information into visual content.

Since social media users often view content quickly, I wanted the infographic to present the main message in a way that was organized, noticeable, and easy to understand.

The design therefore focused on clear information, visual hierarchy, and an organized layout.

---

## Target Audience

The infographic was designed with the intended audience in mind.

I considered how much information could be presented without making the design overwhelming. The content needed to remain understandable while still being visually engaging.

---

# DESIGN CHOICES

## Visual Hierarchy

I emphasized the most important information through size, placement, and visual weight.

This allows viewers to identify the main message before moving toward supporting details.

## Color

The colors were selected to support the message and create visual consistency.

I avoided using colors only for decoration and instead considered how they contributed to the overall communication.

## Typography

Typography was selected with readability in mind.

Different sizes and weights helped separate titles, headings, and supporting information.

## Layout

The information was arranged into sections so that viewers could follow the content naturally.

Spacing was also considered to prevent the infographic from appearing overcrowded.

## Visual Elements

Visual elements were included to support the information and make the content more engaging.

The elements were selected based on their relevance to the message rather than simply adding decoration.

---

# CREATIVE PROCESS

## 1. Idea

I first identified the main message that I wanted the infographic to communicate.

## 2. Planning

I organized the information and determined which parts should receive the most attention.

## 3. Initial Design

I developed the layout and began combining text, colors, typography, and visual elements.

## 4. Review

I examined the initial design to identify areas that needed improvement.

## 5. Revision

I adjusted the layout, spacing, typography, and visual hierarchy to make the design clearer.

## 6. Final Output

After completing the revisions, I finalized the infographic and prepared the mini project documentation.

---

## Why I Chose This Approach

I chose this approach because an infographic needs to communicate information efficiently.

A design can be visually attractive but still ineffective if the audience cannot understand its message.

By organizing the content and controlling the visual hierarchy, I was able to focus on both appearance and communication.

---

## MINI PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

The mini project documentation records the development of the activity, including the concept, design decisions, creative process, and final output.

It shows how the initial idea was transformed into a completed visual project through planning and revision.

---

## What I Learned

This activity helped me understand how design principles can be applied to different forms of communication.

I learned that creating an infographic requires more than combining text and graphics. The information must be organized, the visual hierarchy must be clear, and every design choice should support the main message.

The activity also helped me become more comfortable with the process of developing, reviewing, and improving a visual output.
