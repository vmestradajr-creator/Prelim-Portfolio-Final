# 01 — PRESENTATION DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Overview

The first Prelim activity focused on presentation design principles.

For this activity, I created a presentation while applying different principles of visual design. The purpose was to make the presentation more organized, readable, and effective in communicating its information.

Instead of placing information on slides without considering the overall structure, I focused on how each element could contribute to the message of the presentation.

---

## My Concept

The concept of my presentation was based on creating a clear and organized visual experience for the audience.

I wanted the information to be easy to follow while maintaining a visually appealing presentation. The design was therefore planned around simplicity, organization, and consistency.

The main idea was that the audience should be able to identify the important information without being distracted by unnecessary elements.

---

## Design Principles I Applied

### 1. Visual Hierarchy

I used different sizes and levels of emphasis to distinguish titles, headings, and supporting information.

This helped guide the audience's attention toward the most important parts of each slide.

### 2. Alignment

I organized text and visual elements using consistent alignment.

Proper alignment made the slides appear more structured and prevented the content from looking randomly placed.

### 3. Contrast

Contrast was used to make important information stand out.

Differences in size, weight, and visual emphasis helped separate major information from supporting details.

### 4. Balance

I considered the distribution of elements across the slide.

Balanced layouts helped prevent one side of the presentation from appearing too crowded or empty.

### 5. Consistency

I maintained similar design choices throughout the presentation.

Consistent fonts, spacing, colors, and layouts helped make the presentation feel like one complete project.

### 6. Simplicity

I avoided unnecessary elements whenever possible.

Keeping the design simple allowed the audience to focus on the information instead of being distracted by excessive decoration.

---

## How I Developed the Final Output

My development process started by organizing the information that needed to be included in the presentation.

After identifying the important content, I planned how the information could be distributed across the slides. I then worked on the visual arrangement and applied the design principles discussed in the activity.

I reviewed the presentation to check whether the text was readable, the elements were properly arranged, and the slides maintained a consistent appearance.

After reviewing the initial design, I made adjustments to improve the overall presentation.

---

## How the Principles Improved My Presentation

Applying design principles made the presentation more organized and easier to understand.

Instead of treating every element equally, I was able to create a visual hierarchy that directed attention toward important information.

Alignment and consistency also made the slides look more professional, while contrast and simplicity helped improve readability.

---

## What I Learned

This activity taught me that presentation design is not only about making slides attractive.

A well-designed presentation should help the audience understand the message. Design principles provide a structure that allows information to be presented clearly and effectively.

I also learned that reviewing and revising a design is an important part of the creative process.
