## OVERALL REFLECTION

The Prelim activities helped me understand that good design is more than making something visually appealing.

I learned that every design decision has a purpose. The arrangement of information, choice of colors, typography, spacing, and visual elements can affect how an audience understands and responds to a message.

Through these activities, I also became more familiar with the process of developing an idea, organizing it, reviewing the result, and making improvements.

This portfolio represents my work during the Prelim period and my progress in developing my skills in design and visual communication.
