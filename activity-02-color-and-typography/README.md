# 02 — COLOR PALETTE AND TYPOGRAPHY

## Overview

The second Prelim activity focused on the relationship between color and typography in design.

For this activity, I created a color palette and selected typography that could work together as a consistent visual system.

The goal was to understand how these two elements can influence the appearance, mood, readability, and identity of a design.

---

## My Concept

My concept was to create a visual style that is simple, cohesive, and easy to recognize.

Rather than selecting colors and fonts randomly, I considered how they would work together and how they could communicate a specific visual mood.

The final choices were based on balance, readability, and consistency.

---

# COLOR PALETTE

## Primary Color

The primary color was selected to establish the main visual identity of the design.

It provides the strongest visual presence and can be used for important elements such as headings, backgrounds, or major design features.

## Supporting Colors

Supporting colors were selected to complement the primary color.

Their purpose is to create variation without making the overall design feel inconsistent.

## Neutral Colors

Neutral colors can be used for text, backgrounds, and other areas where readability is important.

They help balance stronger colors and provide visual space within the design.

---

## Why I Chose These Colors

I selected the colors based on how well they worked together rather than focusing on a single color alone.

The combination needed to provide enough contrast for readability while maintaining a consistent visual appearance.

I also considered the mood created by the colors and how they could influence the audience's perception of the design.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Typography was another major part of the activity.

I selected fonts based on readability and how well they supported the overall visual identity.

The typography was organized into different levels:

### Title

The title uses the strongest typographic emphasis because it introduces the main subject.

### Heading

Headings separate major sections and help the audience navigate the information.

### Body Text

Body text is kept readable and simple so that longer information can be understood easily.

---

## Why I Selected This Typography

The typography was chosen because it provides a balance between appearance and functionality.

A font may look attractive, but it still needs to be readable. For this reason, I considered factors such as font size, weight, spacing, and hierarchy.

The selected typography also complements the chosen color palette and contributes to a consistent visual identity.

---

## Development Process

I began by identifying the visual style I wanted to achieve.

I then explored different color combinations and considered how they looked together. After narrowing down the palette, I experimented with typography that could complement the colors.

I organized the typography into different levels and considered how each font size and weight would be used.

Finally, I reviewed the overall combination to make sure the colors and typography worked together rather than competing for attention.

## What I Learned

This activity helped me understand that color and typography are important communication tools.

Color can influence mood and attention, while typography can affect readability and establish hierarchy.

I learned that good design requires these elements to work together instead of being selected independently.
